⚠ BREAKING CVE-2026-50656

Microsoft Defender Zero-Day 'RoguePlanet' Vulnerability Confirmed

Microsoft confirms RoguePlanet, a Defender zero-day with CVE-2026-50656, a 7.8 CVSS score privilege escalation flaw.

📈 Impact: This vulnerability allows attackers to gain SYSTEM-level privileges, posing a significant risk to security. Microsoft is working on a patch, but the timeline for release is not specified.

✅ Action: Security teams should monitor Microsoft's updates for a patch to address the RoguePlanet vulnerability and consider mitigations to reduce the risk of exploitation.

⚠ BREAKING CVE-2025-20701

Apple Patches High-Severity Eavesdropping Vulnerability in Beats Studio Buds

Apple patched CVE-2025-20701, a high-severity vulnerability in Beats Studio Buds, allowing nearby attackers to eavesdrop via microphone.

📈 Impact: The vulnerability could be exploited by nearby hackers to eavesdrop on users, and successful exploitation could lead to remote escalation of privilege. This vulnerability affects multiple manufacturers, including Jabra, Bose, and JBL.

✅ Action: Security teams should ensure their Beats Studio Buds are updated to Beats Firmware Update 1B211 to patch the vulnerability.
